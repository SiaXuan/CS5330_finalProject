Methodology

Text-Image Residual Gating

TIRG learns to compose a reference image with a natural-language modification caption to produce a query vector for retrieval — without modifying the frozen FashionCLIP backbone.

gate = Linear(1024→512) + Sigmoid — controls how much of v to keep
residual = Linear→ReLU→Linear — adds the text-guided shift
v = image emb  ·  t = text emb  (both 512-d, FashionCLIP, frozen)
🎯Trained with symmetric InfoNCE loss on Fashion-IQ triplets (reference, caption → target)
🔗Joint training across all three categories (dress / shirt / toptee) for better generalisation
